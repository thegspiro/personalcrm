*Schema: `20260831120000_add_location_history`*

#### Added
- **A Locations view groups visits and people by place.** Existing location
  text is associated during upgrade without being rewritten or discarded, and
  new interaction forms suggest known places while still accepting a new one.
- **Location history follows the privacy lock.** Hidden interactions and
  private participants do not appear in names, visit totals, or people counts.
