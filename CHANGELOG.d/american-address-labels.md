### American labels on the address forms — 2026-09-08

*Schema: none*

#### Changed
- **"Region" now reads "State"** on a person's profile, a contact address, a
  place, and your home base. Only the labels changed: what you have already
  entered is untouched, exports keep their existing column names, and an import
  that maps `region`, `state` or `province` still works exactly as before.
- **The rest of the interface now spells things the American way** —
  favorites, color, recognized — and the sample text in the address and
  household fields uses American examples. Nothing you have typed is affected;
  these are the app's own words, not your data.
