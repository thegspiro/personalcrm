### A calendar for everything dated — 2026-09-05

*Schema: none*

#### Added
- **A calendar.** Under More → Calendar, a month at a time: plans you have
  pencilled in, birthdays and important dates, follow-ups that are due, what
  people have on, and everything you have already logged — all on the day they
  belong to. A month grid on a desktop, and the same month as a day-by-day
  agenda on a phone. Tapping anything goes to it; nothing is created or edited
  from the grid itself. Every day is worked out in your own timezone, so
  something logged at half past eleven at night lands on that night rather than
  on the next morning, and a date nobody can place — "sometime in 2019" — is
  deliberately left off, because there is no honest square for it and putting
  it on a particular Tuesday would make a vague memory look certain.
- **"Weeks start on" does something now.** The setting has been stored and read
  by nothing, because there was no grid to order. It sets the calendar's first
  column, and the control is back in Settings → Appearance.
