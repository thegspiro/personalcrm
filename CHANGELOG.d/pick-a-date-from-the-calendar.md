### Pick a date from a calendar — 2026-09-12

*Schema: none*

#### Changed
- **The "When" field on anything you log now opens a real calendar.** The small
  calendar mark at the end of the box was the browser's own, not the app's: it
  was a few millimetres wide, it looked and behaved differently in every
  browser, and in some it did nothing at all — so in practice the date had to be
  typed. It is now a button of its own that opens a month you can click a day
  in, with the time beside it and the same Now / −1 day / −1 week / −1 month
  shortcuts underneath. Typing straight into the box still works exactly as
  before, and what gets saved is unchanged.
- **The calendar can be used without a mouse.** Tab reaches the month, the
  arrow keys walk it a day or a week at a time and follow into the next month
  at the edges, Page Up and Page Down step whole months, and Enter chooses the
  day. It opens on the day already selected rather than on the first control.
