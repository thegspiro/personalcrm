### Shared family context — 2026-08-31

*Schema: `20260831120000_add_shared_family_context`*

#### Added
- **One significant moment can now belong to several people.** A marriage,
  move, birth, reunion, or bereavement is stored once and appears on every
  selected profile; existing moments are preserved and backfilled automatically.
- **Interactions distinguish attendees from people discussed.** Mentioning a
  relative makes the conversation discoverable from their history without
  falsely moving their last-contact date.
- **Family links can carry context from the moment they are created**, including
  co-parenting arrangements and other details that do not fit in a label.

#### Changed
- **Logging from a person's profile keeps them selected but lets you add other
  attendees**, so family meals, calls, and celebrations do not need duplicate
  entries.
