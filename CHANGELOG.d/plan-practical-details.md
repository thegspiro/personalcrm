### Plans you can actually act on — 2026-08-30

*Schema: `20260830120000_expand_plan_practical_details`*

#### Changed
- **Plan editing is grouped into Idea, When and where, and Practical details**
  rather than one flat list of fields.
- **A plan's city becomes a complete address.** The existing city text is
  carried into the wider field by the migration, so nothing recorded is lost —
  "Austin" simply becomes the address it always was.

#### Added
- **An editable checklist on a plan**, with starter suggestions that arrive
  unchecked. Nothing is ticked on your behalf, and existing plans start with an
  empty list rather than inheriting work nobody agreed to.
