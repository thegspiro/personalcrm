### People in their life — 2026-09-05

*Schema: `20260905153056_add_associates`*

#### Added

- **A person's page now has a place for the people in *their* life.** The
  colleague they keep mentioning, a sibling's partner, the friend whose new job
  you meant to ask about — a name, how they know each other, and a note. These
  are deliberately not contacts of your own: they get no cadence, never appear
  in your people list, and can never show up as someone you have failed to keep
  in touch with.
- **A roll-up of all of them** under People → Their people, grouped by the
  person whose life they are in. The main navigation is unchanged.
- **One of them can be promoted into someone you do track.** A short form
  confirms the name and how the two are related; it creates the person, carries
  the note into their profile summary, and records the relationship both ways.
  The original entry is kept as a record of what you wrote at the time, marked
  as tracked and linked to the new profile rather than deleted. Deleting that
  person later leaves the note behind, editable again.
- **Searching people now matches these names too**, so looking for the friend
  finds the person who knows them.

#### Changed

- **An entry can be marked private on its own**, independently of the person it
  belongs to, and is withheld while the lock is closed either way. As with
  facts and debts, an account holding a private entry is no longer offered
  offline caching — marking your first one will switch that option off.
