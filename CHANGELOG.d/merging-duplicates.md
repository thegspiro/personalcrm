### One person, one record — 2026-09-08

*Schema: `20260908120000_add_duplicate_dismissal`*

#### Added
- **Settings → Data now finds people who might be the same person**, and can
  fold one into the other. Importing a contacts file twice is the usual way to
  end up with two of somebody, and until now the only remedy was deleting one —
  which took its history with it.
- **Merging keeps everything.** Interactions, dates, gifts, debts, notes, tags,
  households, plans, follow-ups and anything you have added yourself all move to
  the record you keep. Where both records answer the same question differently —
  two birthdays, two jobs — you are asked which answer to keep, one field at a
  time. Anything only one of them answered simply fills in.
- **"Not the same person"** puts a pair away for good. Two people genuinely can
  share a phone number, and a suggestion you cannot refuse is one you learn to
  ignore.

#### Notes
- **The match is on a shared email address or phone number, never on a name.**
  Two people with the same name are usually two people, and this cannot be
  undone. It will not find "Bob" and "Robert" unless they share a detail.
- **Merging is permanent.** The record you do not keep is deleted once
  everything has been moved off it, which the screen says before you commit.
- **If either record is private, the merged one is.** That is not offered as a
  choice: the alternative would publish everything the private record carried.
- Two records that both have a dating profile are refused rather than merged,
  since one profile would have to be discarded. Remove the one you do not want
  first.
