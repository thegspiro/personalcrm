### Unified follow-ups — 2026-09-02

*Schema: none*

#### Changed

- **Follow-ups now brings both kinds of outstanding action together.** The page
  keeps due contact cadences under “People to contact” and manual commitments
  under “Things to do,” without combining or changing their stored records.
- **A person's own list of commitments is now called “Tasks.”** “Follow-ups” is
  the hub that holds both those tasks and the people you are due to contact, so
  the two no longer share a name. Deep links from the dashboard land on
  `#tasks` on the contact page.
