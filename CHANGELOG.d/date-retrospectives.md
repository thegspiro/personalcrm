### Looking back on a date — 2026-08-30

*Schema: `20260830120000_add_date_entry_retrospective`*

#### Added
- **A date can be reflected on after it happens.** The notes are optional and
  separate from the preparation notes written beforehand, so what you thought
  afterwards is never mixed into what you planned.
- **"Past dates worth repeating"** collects the ones that went well and can copy
  their logistics into a new plan. Only the logistics travel: private commentary
  stays where it was written, and nothing crosses between accounts.
