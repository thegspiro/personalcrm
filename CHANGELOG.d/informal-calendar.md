### Informal calendar information — 2026-09-03

*Schema: `20260903120000_add_happenings`*

#### Added
- **A place to record what someone else has on.** People rarely want to share a
  calendar, but "she is in Portugal from the 12th" is worth knowing twice: once
  before, so you do not invite her to something she will miss, and once after,
  so you remember to ask how it went. Each entry sits on the person's profile
  under *Going on in their life* with its dates, an optional *Away* or *Busy*
  marker, a *Maybe* marker for anything you only half-heard, and a note of how
  you came by it.
- **A "In their world" dashboard widget**, enabled by default, showing what is
  coming up across everyone you know and what has just wrapped up and is worth
  asking about. Existing dashboard layouts pick it up automatically and it can
  be reordered or switched off like any other widget.
- **An optional follow-up reminder.** Tick *Remind me to ask how it went* and an
  ordinary task appears, due the day after the thing ends — so it reaches the
  tasks page, the daily digest and whichever reminder channels you already have
  configured. Editing the dates moves that task rather than adding another.
  Clearing the box removes it only if you have not already ticked it off.
- **Vague dates stay vague.** "Sometime in October" is stored as the month, and
  the follow-up waits until November rather than asking on the 2nd about a trip
  not yet taken.
- **A new *Going on in their life* type list** under Settings → Tags & types —
  trips, work travel, visitors staying, medical, deadlines and the rest — which
  you can rename, recolour and add to. Existing accounts get the starter list on
  the next restart; no manual step.

Entries for someone marked private are withheld from the profile and the widget
alike while the privacy lock is closed, and offline caching is unaffected.
