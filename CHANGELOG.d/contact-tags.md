### Contact tags — 2026-09-02

*Schema: none*

#### Added

- **Tags can now be assigned on contact forms, used to filter people, and managed in Settings.** Renaming preserves assignments, merging combines and deduplicates them, and deleting a tag never deletes a contact.

#### Fixed

- **Tag lists and usage totals respect the privacy lock and account owner.** A locked private contact cannot be inferred from a tag name or count.
